# Florida Gators: "Football History vs Louisiana State University" (study copy)

A frozen copy of the mobile floridagators.com Opponent History page, made for a private, non-commercial usability
study (family "sports"). Not for redistribution or any other use.

- **Source:** https://floridagators.com/sports/football/opponent-history/louisiana-state-university/34, captured on 2026-10-27 at 390 CSS px with an iPhone user agent.
- **Rights:** the page design, text, photos, video stills and the Florida Gators marks are (c) the University of Florida
  Athletic Association, LEARFIELD and Sidearm Sports; the opponent's marks belong to that school; the cookie notice is
  Transcend's; ad creatives belong to their advertisers. Game dates and scores are facts. The site's Adobe Fonts
  (Typekit) files are self-hosted for the study so text wraps the same on every phone.
- **Changes:** site scripts, trackers and live ad tags removed; the cookie notice (with its close, language and
  "Do Not Sell" views), the header menus, the calendar's tabs and sport filter, the games' "Links" menus, the table's
  sideways-scroll shadows, the video pop-ups and the ads' lazy appearance and "Hide advertisement" button re-created
  by local scripts (site/page.js); ads frozen to one creative per slot; video streams replaced by a picture of the
  player; all files stored locally. In the study's adapted condition, WebAble changes the page's presentation.
