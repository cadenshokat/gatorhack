# National Weather Service: 7-day forecast for Gainesville, FL (study copy)

A frozen copy of the NWS point forecast page as a phone sees it, made for a private, non-commercial usability study
(the practice page, always shown as the site made it).

- **Source:** https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?CityName=Gainesville&state=FL&site=JAX&textField1=29.6742&textField2=-82.3363
  (where a search for "Gainesville, FL" on weather.gov lands), captured 2026-10-27 with an iPhone user agent at
  390 CSS px. Forecast issued 4:26 pm EDT, 27 Oct 2026, by NWS Jacksonville, FL.
- **Forecast text, icons, radar, satellite and forecast graphics:** works of the US federal government (NOAA / National
  Weather Service), public domain in the US (17 U.S.C. 105). Credit: National Weather Service.
- **NOAA and NWS emblems:** government insignia. They appear only because they are part of the copied page inside a
  private research study; no endorsement is implied.
- **Map tiles:** (c) Esri and its data providers ("Tiles (c) ESRI" on the page), kept as captured for a
  non-commercial research copy.
- **CSS and icon fonts (self-hosted from the page's own CSS):** Bootstrap 3 (MIT, Glyphicons Halflings bundled with
  it), Font Awesome 4 (SIL OFL 1.1, CSS MIT), OpenLayers (BSD-2-Clause) and NWS styles.
- **Changes:** site scripts and trackers removed; the menu button and the menu's dropdowns re-created by local scripts
  (`site/page.js` for the dropdowns); "View in Desktop Mode" left inert; the map frozen as captured; all files stored
  locally.
