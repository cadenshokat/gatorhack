Refill questions

When will my refill be ready?

Most refills are ready in about 2 hours. You'll get a text or email when your order is ready. Some medicines need to be ordered and can take 1 to 2 business days.

Can someone else pick up my prescription?

Yes. The person picking up will need your name and date of birth, and may be asked for a photo ID.

What does "Too soon to refill" mean?

Your insurance sets the earliest date a refill can be filled. We show that date next to the prescription and can remind you when it's available.

How do I transfer a prescription from another pharmacy?

Use Transfer a prescription and tell us the medicine and the other pharmacy's phone number. We'll handle the rest.
