WebAble demo pages

Two fictional sites for showing WebAble at Gator Hack V. They are plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, work offline, and contain nothing written for WebAble.

Local pages

Main demo

Alderra Pharmacy: refill

A busy refill page. The real action, "Confirm refill", is a 104 × 28 px grey button below the fold, after a loud "Continue shopping".

  • Self-rotating promo carousel, no pause
  • Flu-shot and membership upsells inside the form
  • 11 px grey fine print, cookie banner, chat that opens at 4 s
Open the refill page
?reset=1 · fresh visit ?modal=1 · text signup

Lakeshore County: benefits application

A long form with a session-timeout warning that has to stay and a survey popup that can be put away.

  • Session warning with a live countdown
  • Survey invitation in the corner
  • Required fields, filled fields and a help line
Open the application
?timeout=0 · no warning ?survey=0 · no survey ?minutes=1 · short countdown
